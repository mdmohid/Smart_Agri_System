Frequently Asked Questions

🌾 What is AgriSmart?

AgriSmart is an AI‑powered advisory system that helps farmers with crop recommendations, disease detection, yield prediction, and market prices.

📱 Is there a mobile app?

Currently we offer a responsive web app. A mobile app is planned for future releases.

💵 Is the service free?

Basic features are free. Premium plans with advanced analytics will be available soon.

🛠 How accurate are the predictions?

Our models are trained on real agricultural data and achieve >85% accuracy in field tests.
